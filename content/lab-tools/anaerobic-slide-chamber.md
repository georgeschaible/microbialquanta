Live cell imaging of obligate anaerobes is difficult because the moment the slide leaves the glove box, oxygen exposure changes the experiment. This chamber holds a standard glass slide between two printed halves with a coverslip top and bottom, sealed with a rubber O ring in a printed groove. Gas flows in one barb and out the other, so you can flush with nitrogen, argon, or a defined gas mix and image for as long as the flow keeps up. The barbs print as part of the chamber, no fittings to source.
