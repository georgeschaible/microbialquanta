Commercial gas manifolds for anaerobic work cost several hundred dollars. This is the same idea built from a printed backplate and four disposable three way stopcocks, total cost under fifteen dollars. The clips hold the valves firmly enough to operate one handed without the manifold flexing, and the whole thing mounts to the wall above the bench with two screws.
