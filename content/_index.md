intro_headline: "I'm George. I study the microbes we haven't yet learned to grow in the lab."
intro_paragraphs:
  - "Most of what we know about microbial physiology comes from a handful of organisms that grow well in pure culture. The interesting biology is in the rest, the uncultured majority that we can only study in place."
  - "My work uses single-cell techniques, including Raman microspectroscopy, FISH, SIP-NanoSIMS, and correlative electron microscopy, to trace how individual cells participate in the chemistry of their ecosystems. <em>Thiovulum</em>, a large and unusually fast-swimming sulfur-oxidizing bacterium, has been a recent focus."
  - "Alongside the science I build instruments. 3D printed micromanipulators, Raspberry Pi data loggers, and custom microscopy setups. Everything is shared openly, because the cost of scientific tools shouldn't be the bottleneck on what science gets done."
